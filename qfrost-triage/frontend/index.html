QFrost Triage

"Every circuit has bugs. Some bill per shot."

A static risk and vulnerability scanner for Qiskit circuits. Discovers quantified weaknesses (QWEs), assigns CVSS-for-quantum severity scores, and outputs SARIF reports for CI/CD gates. Zero QPU execution, zero shots billed.

The Scoring Rule: "CVSS for Quantum"

Quantified, logarithmic risk scoring derived from hardware physics budgets.

score = clamp(1.0 + 9.0 * log10(max_ratio) / log10(cap), 1.0, 10.0)

Where max_ratio is the headline cost multiplier relative to the engineering threshold, and cap is the ratio mapping to 10.0.

≥ 9.0 CRITICAL ≥ 7.0 HIGH ≥ 4.0 MEDIUM ≥ 2.0 LOW < 2.0 INFO
