# QWE-007: Idle Qubits During Long Gates

## Overview
- **Identifier**: QWE-007
- **Title**: Idle Qubits During Long Gates (Unmitigated Spectator Dephasing & Crosstalk)
- **Baseline Severity**: LOW
- **CVSS for Quantum Formula**:
  $$\text{score} = \text{clamp}\left(1 + 9 \cdot \frac{\log_{10}(\text{max\_ratio})}{\log_{10}(\text{cap})}, 1.0, 10.0\right)$$
  - Headline metric: $\text{idle\_ratio} = \frac{T_{\text{idle\_spectator}}}{T_{\text{critical\_path}}}$
  - $\text{budget} = 0.30$ (Idle time $> 30\%$ of total circuit execution duration)
  - $\text{cap} = 3.0$
  - Severity: LOW if idle ratio exceeds 30%, MEDIUM if idle ratio exceeds 60%, INFO if $\le 30\%$.

## What This Weakness Is
In heterogeneous quantum circuits, two-qubit operations (e.g. cross-resonance CNOT gates or CZ gates) take approximately $200\text{ ns}$, whereas single-qubit rotations take $\sim 20\text{ ns}$.

When one subsystem undergoes a deep sequential chain of two-qubit interactions while spectator qubits have completed their initial gates and remain idle, spectator qubits sit exposed to environmental bath interactions:
- **Phase Dephasing ($T_2^*$)**: Idling qubits accumulate random phase drifts.
- **$ZZ$ Crosstalk**: Residual always-on dispersive coupling with neighboring active qubits shifts transition frequencies.

Without active scheduling or dynamical decoupling, spectator qubits suffer significant state fidelity decay before their next interaction.

## Why It Costs Money and Time on Quantum Hardware
- **Uneven Fidelity Erosion**: Algorithms relying on balanced entanglement (such as quantum teleportation or distributed entanglement purification) fail because spectator halves degrade while waiting for slow gates.
- **Lost Coherence Margins**: Unscheduled idle gaps consume precious coherence time that could otherwise support additional computational depth.

## The Quantum Exploit Path
1. Circuit schedules deep serial entangling gates on a subset of qubits ($q_0, q_1$).
2. Spectator qubits ($q_2 \dots q_n$) remain unpulsed for $> 500\text{ ns}$.
3. Ambient dephasing and crosstalk degrade the spectator quantum state.
4. Downstream parity or interference measurements return degraded contrast.

## Mitigations
1. **Dynamical Decoupling (DD)**: Insert symmetric CPMG or XY4 pulse sequences ($X_{\pi} - Y_{\pi} - X_{\pi} - Y_{\pi}$) in spectator idle windows to refocus environmental dephasing.
2. **ASAP / ALAP Schedule Balancing**: Align gate execution using As-Late-As-Possible (ALAP) scheduling so spectator qubits remain in the ground state $|0\rangle$ (which has infinite dephasing protection) until immediately prior to computation.
3. **Crosstalk-Aware Gate Scheduling**: Stagger concurrent two-qubit drives on adjacent frequency channels.
