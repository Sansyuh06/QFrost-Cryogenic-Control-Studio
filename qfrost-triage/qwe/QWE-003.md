# QWE-003: Unmitigated Readout Bias

## Overview
- **Identifier**: QWE-003
- **Title**: Unmitigated Readout Bias (State Measurement Assignment Error Decay)
- **Baseline Severity**: HIGH
- **CVSS for Quantum Formula**:
  $$\text{score} = \text{clamp}\left(1 + 9 \cdot \frac{\log_{10}(\text{max\_ratio})}{\log_{10}(\text{cap})}, 1.0, 10.0\right)$$
  - Headline metric: $\text{misread\_ratio} = \frac{P_{\text{misread}}}{P_{\text{budget}}}$
  - $P_{\text{misread}} = 1 - (1 - p_{\text{RO}})^N$ (where $N = \text{number of measured qubits}$, $p_{\text{RO}} = 0.015$)
  - $P_{\text{budget}} = 0.05$ (5% expected misread probability)
  - $\text{cap} = 10.0$ ($50\%$ misread probability maps to score 10.0)
  - Severity: HIGH when measured qubits $N \ge 6$ and expected misread $> 5.0\%$, CRITICAL if expected misread $> 30\%$, MEDIUM if $N \ge 3$ and $> 3\%$.

## What This Weakness Is
Quantum measurement on physical architectures relies on dispersive readout via coupled microwave resonators (in transmons) or fluorescence detection (in trapped ions). Readout assignment errors arise from thermal state transitions during measurement, ring-up latency, and overlapping state discrimination IQ clouds.

When measuring an $N$-qubit register simultaneously, the probability that *at least one bit flip occurs* in the measured bitstring scales exponentially with register width:
$$P_{\text{misread}} = 1 - (1 - p_{\text{RO}})^N$$
For a conservative per-qubit error $p_{\text{RO}} = 1.5\%$:
- $N = 4 \implies P_{\text{misread}} = 5.8\%$
- $N = 8 \implies P_{\text{misread}} = 11.4\%$
- $N = 16 \implies P_{\text{misread}} = 21.6\%$
- $N = 32 \implies P_{\text{misread}} = 38.6\%$

Without classical readout error mitigation (such as M3 matrix-free inversion or T-REX twirling), raw histograms represent a heavily corrupted probability distribution.

## Why It Costs Money and Time on Quantum Hardware
- **Corrupted Sampling Statistics**: The majority of experimental quantum algorithms (VQE, QAOA, phase estimation) compute ground state energies or optimal cost functions from bitstring parity and expectation values. Unmitigated readout bias shifts calculated expectation values away from physical ground truth.
- **Wasted Shots**: Users often compensate for noisy distributions by requesting $100,000+$ shots, mistakenly believing shot noise is the limiting factor, thereby burning cloud credits on systematic hardware bias that more shots cannot eliminate.

## The Quantum Exploit Path
1. Circuit measures $\ge 6$ qubits directly to classical registers.
2. No readout calibration or mitigation pass is scheduled or configured in the runtime options.
3. The raw shot counts yield biased parity expectation values, leading VQE optimizers to converge to incorrect variational minima.

## Mitigations
1. **Matrix-Free Measurement Mitigation (M3)**: Direct inversion within the subspace of sampled bitstrings, avoiding full $2^N \times 2^N$ assignment matrix inversion.
2. **Twirled Readout Error eXtinguishing (T-REX)**: Twirl readout errors into a Pauli channel using random bit flips (X gates prior to measurement inverted in software), decoupling error from circuit state.
3. **Iterative Unfolding / Calibration Matrices**: Compute and invert local 1-qubit confusion matrices on measured subsets.
