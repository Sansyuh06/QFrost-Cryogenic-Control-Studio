# QWE-005: Decoder Budget Blowout

## Overview
- **Identifier**: QWE-005
- **Title**: Decoder Budget Blowout (Classical Surface-Code Syndrome Decoding Exceeds 1-Microsecond Budget)
- **Baseline Severity**: INFO / MEDIUM (Roadmap Advisory)
- **Advisory Tag**: `not exploitable pre-2029 — roadmap finding`
- **CVSS for Quantum Formula**:
  $$\text{score} = \text{clamp}\left(1 + 9 \cdot \frac{\log_{10}(\text{max\_ratio})}{\log_{10}(\text{cap})}, 1.0, 10.0\right)$$
  - Headline metric: $\text{cycle\_time\_vs\_budget} = \frac{T_{\text{cycle}}}{T_{\text{budget}}}$
  - $T_{\text{cycle}} = T_{\text{readout}} + 2 \cdot T_{\text{cable}} + T_{\text{decode}}$
  - $T_{\text{budget}} = 1,000\text{ ns}$ (Cryogenic reaction budget)
  - $\text{cap} = 3.0$
  - Severity: INFO if $T_{\text{cycle}} \le 1,000\text{ ns}$ (inside budget), MEDIUM if $> 1,000\text{ ns}$ at room temp, HIGH if $> 2,000\text{ ns}$.

## What This Weakness Is
Fault-tolerant quantum computing based on the surface code requires measuring stabilizer ancillas (X and Z stabilizers) and classical decoding to identify and correct Pauli errors faster than new errors accumulate.

The total round-trip cycle time consists of:
$$T_{\text{cycle}} = T_{\text{readout}} + 2 \cdot T_{\text{cable}} + T_{\text{decode}}$$
Where:
- $T_{\text{readout}} \approx 300\text{ ns}$
- $T_{\text{cable}} \approx 80\text{ ns}$ (for 4K shelf) or $240\text{ ns}$ (for 300K room temp)
- $T_{\text{decode}}$ is the classical algorithm execution time (Union-Find, MWPM, or Neural Decoder).

If $T_{\text{cycle}} > 1,000\text{ ns}$, classical decoding latency outpaces the physical coherence decay rate of the underlying physical qubits, causing an exponential backlog of error syndrome accumulation ("decoder backlog problem").

## Why It Costs Money and Time on Quantum Hardware
- **Roadmap Architectural Bottleneck**: In current NISQ hardware (pre-2029), large-scale active QEC cycles with real-time feedback are not yet running in production; hence this finding is classified as an architectural advisory (`not exploitable pre-2029`).
- **Hardware Sizing**: Designing quantum chips with codes larger than distance $d=5$ while relying on off-chip, room-temperature classical decoders will incur failure upon QEC deployment, requiring expensive hardware control redesigns.

## The Quantum Exploit Path
1. QEC architect targets surface code distance $d \ge 5$.
2. The decoding pipeline is placed on a room-temperature server or FPGA cluster ($T_{\text{cable}} \approx 240\text{ ns}$, round-trip cable delay $\approx 480\text{ ns}$).
3. Classical decoding takes $> 500\text{ ns}$.
4. Combined latency exceeds $1,000\text{ ns}$, violating fault-tolerance thresholds and causing logical error rate breakdown.

## Mitigations
1. **Cryogenic Decoder Co-processors**: Place hardware decoders (ASIC/FPGA) at the 4K stage inside the dilution refrigerator, reducing cable latency from $480\text{ ns}$ to $160\text{ ns}$.
2. **Fast Decoders (Union-Find & Clique Decoders)**: Utilize almost-linear time decoding algorithms (Delfosse & Nickerson) or specialized accelerators (QECOOL, AFS, NISQ+) that solve syndrome graphs in $<300\text{ ns}$.
