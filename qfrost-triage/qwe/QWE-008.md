# QWE-008: Depth Exceeds Coherence Envelope

## Overview
- **Identifier**: QWE-008
- **Title**: Depth Exceeds Coherence Envelope (Circuit Duration Exceeds Dephasing Threshold)
- **Baseline Severity**: MEDIUM
- **CVSS for Quantum Formula**:
  $$\text{score} = \text{clamp}\left(1 + 9 \cdot \frac{\log_{10}(\text{max\_ratio})}{\log_{10}(\text{cap})}, 1.0, 10.0\right)$$
  - Headline metric: $\text{coherence\_ratio} = \frac{T_{\text{circuit}}}{T_2^*}$
  - $T_{\text{circuit}} = \sum (\text{depth}_{1q} \cdot t_{1q} + \text{depth}_{2q} \cdot t_{2q})$
  - Modeled constants: $t_{1q} = 20\text{ ns}$, $t_{2q} = 200\text{ ns}$, $T_2^* = 50,000\text{ ns}$ ($50\ \mu\text{s}$)
  - $\text{budget} = 1.0\times$ (circuit execution duration equals $T_2^*$)
  - $\text{cap} = 4.0$ ($4\times T_2^*$ maps to score 10.0)
  - Severity: MEDIUM when $T_{\text{circuit}} > T_2^*$ ($> 50\ \mu\text{s}$), HIGH when $> 2 \times T_2^*$ ($> 100\ \mu\text{s}$), CRITICAL when $> 4 \times T_2^*$.

## What This Weakness Is
Superconducting transmon qubits operate within physical coherence limits defined by:
- $T_1$ (Energy relaxation time, typical $\approx 80\ \mu\text{s}$)
- $T_2^*$ (Pure dephasing time, typical $\approx 50\ \mu\text{s}$)

As circuit depth grows, the physical time elapsed from the initial state preparation gate to final measurement increases:
$$T_{\text{circuit}} \approx d_{1q} \cdot t_{1q} + d_{2q} \cdot t_{2q}$$
When total circuit duration $T_{\text{circuit}}$ exceeds $T_2^*$, off-diagonal elements in the system density matrix decay by $e^{-T_{\text{circuit}} / T_2^*}$. By the time the final measurement pulse arrives, phase coherence has dissolved, leaving an output distribution biased toward thermal equilibrium or uniform white noise.

## Why It Costs Money and Time on Quantum Hardware
- **Zero Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR)**: Beyond $T_2^*$, circuits yield nearly flat random bitstrings. Spending thousands of dollars on cloud QPU time produces no usable algorithmic data.
- **Transpilation Blindness**: High-level algorithms may look reasonable in gate count, but after compiler routing and unrolling, the critical-path duration silently breaches coherence limits.

## The Quantum Exploit Path
1. Algorithm contains deep Trotterized Hamiltonian simulation steps or unoptimized variational repetitions.
2. Two-qubit depth exceeds 250 stages ($250 \times 200\text{ ns} = 50,000\text{ ns} = T_2^*$).
3. The job runs on hardware; measurement outcomes register zero quantum contrast.
4. Experimenter wastes cloud budget re-running shots under the assumption of statistical noise.

## Mitigations
1. **Pulse-Level Optimization**: Compress gate sequences using numerical optimal control (GRAPE / CRAB) or KAK block synthesis.
2. **Circuit Cutting / Partitioning**: Divide deep circuits into shallower sub-circuits evaluated with circuit knitting (subject to QWE-001 budgets).
3. **Hardware Selection**: Route high-depth circuits to long-coherence platforms (such as trapped-ion processors like IonQ with coherence times measured in seconds) rather than transmon systems.
